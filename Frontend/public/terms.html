Free Beta Terms

Last updated: September 21, 2026

WebWatch is an early beta uptime-monitoring service. Check timing and availability are provided on a best-effort basis, and the service does not currently offer a guaranteed service level.

You may monitor only public websites and APIs that you own or are authorized to test. Do not use WebWatch to probe private networks, attack services, or violate another service’s terms.

The beta may change, pause, or end as the product develops. Keep another monitoring method for systems where missed or delayed alerts could cause material harm.

Questions can be sent through the project owner’s GitHub profile.
