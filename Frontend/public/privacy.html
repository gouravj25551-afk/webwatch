Privacy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

WebWatch stores the email address you use for your account, a securely hashed password, the public URLs you choose to monitor, check results, and incident history. This information is used to operate the uptime-monitoring service.

Infrastructure providers such as Vercel and Neon process data to host the application and database. WebWatch does not sell personal information.

You can delete individual monitors and their history from the dashboard. For an account deletion request or privacy question, contact the project owner through their GitHub profile.

Do not submit private, internal, or sensitive URLs. WebWatch is designed to check public websites and APIs.
